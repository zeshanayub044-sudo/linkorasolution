The problem we address

Pages can attract the wrong searches, compete with each other or bury the information a customer needs. Changing titles alone rarely fixes an unclear page purpose or a weak internal journey.

What the service includes

We review important pages, map keywords to intent and recommend practical improvements to content, headings, metadata, links and technical presentation. The scope distinguishes recommendations from implementation and accounts for your CMS and approval process.

Clear deliverables

  • Keyword-to-page map and competitor content review
  • Title tags, meta descriptions and logical heading recommendations
  • Content improvements matched to user intent and conversion goals
  • Internal-link, URL and image-optimization recommendations
  • Applicable structured-data review and Core Web Vitals assessment
  • Prioritized implementation and validation checklist

Our process

  1. Audit — identify page purpose, current content gaps and implementation constraints.
  2. Map — assign search intent and a primary focus to each target page.
  3. Improve — update or recommend copy, metadata, links and structured data.
  4. Validate — check rendered pages, crawlability and the agreed implementation list.

Benefits that matter

Clearer page purpose and structure help visitors assess your service and help search engines interpret your content. The aim is improved relevance and a more useful conversion path, without promising a particular ranking or revenue result.

Who it is for

For local businesses, eCommerce stores, SaaS teams, agencies and service companies. It is particularly useful after a redesign, when new services launch or when important pages overlap in search intent.

Questions before you start

Which packages already exist?

The Services page lists Starter at PKR 15,000 one-time, Growth at PKR 35,000 one-time, and Authority at PKR 70,000 one-time or PKR 25,000/month. Confirm scope and suitability before ordering.

Does on-page SEO include development?

Only if agreed in the scope. Confirm which fixes will be implemented, which are recommendations and who approves changes in your CMS.

Do meta descriptions directly guarantee more traffic?

No. They describe a page for searchers, and search engines may show different snippets. Relevance, competition and many other factors affect traffic.

How do you measure progress?

Use an agreed baseline and review completed changes alongside relevant search queries, qualified visits and conversion behavior. Measurement depends on approved access to your tools.
