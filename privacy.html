Website inquiry data

When you submit a quote request, the details you provide are stored in our connected Supabase project so authorized management can review and respond. Optional campaign parameters from the referring URL are included with the inquiry. Please avoid sending passwords or confidential client information.

What is collected

Name, email, selected service and message are required. Phone, company, website and budget are optional. A short-lived salted hash derived from the request IP helps limit abusive submissions; the raw IP is not stored in the lead table. Hosting and infrastructure providers may keep their own operational logs.

How information is used

Inquiry data is used to assess requirements and communicate with you. Public users cannot read submitted leads. A separately configured CRM may receive inquiry details through a server-side connection; CRM delivery is disabled until that connection is configured.

Google Analytics and your choice

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures public page views, page titles and controlled service/contact interactions. The Google tag loads only after you allow analytics. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. Google Analytics may use analytics cookies to distinguish visits and collect browser/device and interaction information. Google processes the requests it receives under its own privacy policy.

Our event parameters exclude inquiry/application fields, names, contact details, messages, CV contents, passwords and private Employee/Admin activity. Page URLs omit query strings and fragments, except the public article or job identifier used by older links. Referrers exclude queries and private page paths. Campaign parameters stay in session storage for attribution to a submitted inquiry and may be carried on public-page links when opening a new tab; they are not included in our analytics events.

Choose Allow analytics or Decline analytics in the notice. You can reopen Analytics preferences in the footer to withdraw or change your choice. Withdrawal stops further analytics collection and removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies; it cannot retract data Google already received. Your choice is stored locally. If storage is blocked, it applies only in the current tab and later pages default to no analytics. See Google's privacy information.

Questions and data requests

Contact linkoraseosolutions@gmail.com about your inquiry or to request correction or deletion. Management should retain inquiry data only as long as needed and review it periodically. No fixed retention period or legal registration details are asserted here.
