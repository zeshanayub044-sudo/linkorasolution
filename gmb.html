The problem we address

Inconsistent contact details, unclear services or outdated photos can make it harder for customers to choose a business. Local visibility is also affected by factors an agency cannot control, including the searcher’s location and competition.

What the service includes

We review profile completeness, business details, category fit, services, images and posts. Recommendations should reflect Google’s eligibility rules and your actual operations. We can guide review requests; we cannot control customer opinions or guarantee Google Maps positions.

Clear deliverables

  • Profile and eligibility review based on your actual business
  • Business information, category, service and description recommendations
  • Local keyword and citation-consistency review
  • Photo and post planning using approved business assets
  • Authentic review-request and response guidance
  • Prioritized local visibility and inquiry-tracking recommendations

Our process

  1. Confirm — verify business details, eligibility and appropriate owner-managed access.
  2. Audit — assess the profile and consistency of publicly listed information.
  3. Optimize — implement approved categories, service descriptions and assets.
  4. Maintain — review updates, customer questions and the agreed performance signals.

Benefits that matter

Accurate, well-maintained information supports customer confidence and can make calls, directions and website visits easier. No guaranteed Maps position, review score or lead count is implied.

Questions before you start

Can you guarantee a top-three Maps position?

No. Google’s local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, among other factors. We focus on accurate information and useful profile improvements.

Will I retain ownership of my profile?

You should retain ownership and grant only the access needed for agreed work. Do not share your Google password with an agency.

Can you remove negative reviews or create positive ones?

No. We provide guidance for authentic review requests and appropriate responses. Ineligible content can be reported through Google’s process, but removal is not guaranteed.

Is Google Business Profile different from GMB?

Google Business Profile is the current product name. GMB is the older Google My Business abbreviation that many businesses still use.
